Driver check-ins

A short email to every driver once a week, from your HR, with a link to a 1-minute page. Answers land in Reviews.

On for 128 drivers with loads in CargoETL. Last sent Fri Oct 24, 4:00 PM · 46 answered (36%).

How it runs

Each step can be turned off. Messages are ready to go, edit them only if you want.

1
First check-in
After the driver's first load in CargoETL, on the next send day. Introduces you by name.
Edit message
2
Weekly check-in
Every week to drivers who had at least one load. Friday works best: settlement has just landed.
Edit message
3
Quiet week
No loads this week: "Everything okay?" with 4 one-tap answers. "Want more loads" and "Thinking of leaving" go to Call today.
Edit message
4
"Did we fix it?"
If a driver complained last week, the next check-in starts with this question. Automatic.
5
Bad answer alert
1–2 stars, dispatcher "Not great", settlement "No" or "Thinking of leaving" → instant alert and a line in Call today. Reminder if nobody marks "Talked" in 24 h.
6
Google review ask
On the thank-you screen, every week, to every driver who answered (Google does not allow asking only happy drivers). After a good week it comes with ready-to-copy lines built from the driver's own answers.
7
Monday summary
Who to call, dispatchers, what hurts, what got fixed. By email and on the Reviews page.
8
Dispatcher summary
Each dispatcher gets their own ratings with driver names, Monday morning. Free-text notes stay with HR.
9
Don't wear drivers out
No answer for 3 weeks → every 2 weeks. "Pause for a month" link in every email. Inactive units stop getting emails.
